Modifications for this portfolio:
- Converted the coupe body to an open-top convertible by removing roof, side
  and rear glass, B/C pillars, and rear wing.
- Preserved exterior UV coordinates, smooth normals, and textured details.
- Replaced the coupe's cabin shell with an originally authored four-seat
  black-leather interior, dashboard, gauges, steering wheel, and controls.
- Adjusted Macao Blue-inspired navy metallic paint and windshield materials
  for the site's studio lighting.
- Optimized opaque embedded textures to JPEG quality 92 at their original
  1024-pixel dimensions; this includes normal maps.
- Reoriented and centered the model for a rotating web presentation.
